Thank you for joining the WCE SME Technical Review and Open Workshop. The week brings the Subsea Superintendents together to work hands on with the tools the fleet now reports through, to review the WCE technical standards with an external specialist, and to agree a prioritised action list with owners and dates.

Shape of the week

  • Days 1 and 2, training review. Hands on with the reporting tools (WCGRRT and SSORT), the SACRED dashboards, the precharge process and the Argo Navis maintenance planning tool. Everyone posts real reports on the training test asset.
  • Days 3 and 4, open forum. Technical standards with Andrew Dingee, the API Std 53 gap analysis and BSEE, TOMs, CBM lessons learned, SACRED as the governed repository, and the action list.

Intended outcome

A clear, prioritised action list with agreed ownership and next steps, and a clear, detailed direction for the Well Control Equipment and MPD Seminar in Q1/Q2 2027.

Every session ends by adding to one list: the action, the owner, the date.

Arrival

  • Please arrive by 07:45 on Monday 19 October for sign-in and the site safety induction. The opening starts at 08:00 sharp.
  • Venue: Seadrill, 4425 Westway Park Blvd, Suite 170, Houston, TX 77041. Report to reception.
  • Each day runs 08:00 to 17:00, with breaks at 10:00 and 14:30 and lunch at 12:00.

Please bring

  • Your Seadrill laptop, charged, with its charger, and with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome up to date.
  • Your Seadrill network sign-in working (VPN if you are off the office network). The tools and dashboards are served from the Seadrill network.
  • Access to the WellControl SharePoint site. Please open it once before Monday to confirm.
  • Your own examples: a recent rig visit, BOP test, CBM or maintenance plan you would like to discuss.

Hands-on sessions

  • All practical work is done on the SSCE Equipment training asset. It never appears in fleet counts, notifications for it go to the office only, and the class's posts are removed afterwards.
  • The precharge session uses training well names and posts nothing.
  • The Argo Navis session uses a test plan in test mode. Nothing reaches a rig.

Before you travel

  • Travel and accommodation through your usual Seadrill travel arrangements.
  • No pre-reading is required. A trainee workbook is issued on the day.
  • Please think about one task that takes longer than it should on the rig today. It is the starting point for Thursday's efficiency forum.

Hold pointDaily reporting requirements (15 minutes, discussion). At the point in the exercise where the first daily report is posted, the session stops to agree the requirement:
  • When and by whom: the rig posts the Daily Summary Report twice a day, after each shift handover, from Argo Navis. The planner no longer issues it. Who on the rig posts it, and what happens when a report is late.
  • What is inside: the project header (rig, BOP, project type, data date and time, rig manager, OIM, TSL, DSL, subsea supervisor); maintenance day; original and current finish; plan variance and float to the BOP need date in hours; critical path and SIMOPS; project % and BOP % complete; milestones (baseline, estimated, actual); break-in work and delays; quality and rig reliability actions; lessons learned; work completed in the last cycle and planned for the next; general comments.
  • What has to be correct before it is posted: the progress and actual finishes, any break-in work raised, and the people on board for the crew in place.
  • Who it goes to: automatically by email, with the PDF attached and a link to the full schedule on the BOP Fleet Planning page: To the rig manager, assistant rig manager, subsea supervisor, TSL, DSL, electrical supervisor and MPD supervisor of the rig, and the WCE office leads; Cc all TSC WCE Managers, Superintendents and Planners. A line also goes in the plan's Teams chat.
