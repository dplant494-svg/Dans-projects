### Before you start

1. **SAY:** "This afternoon I build one of each report, start to finish, and you follow on your laptop. Type along if it helps, but **don't press Post, Post to OEM or Save to File**. Everything I do is in your manual, step by step, with the picture."
2. **Your dates:** use **2026-10-11** for all four, with serial **TRN-11** on the CBM. Nobody else posts, so nothing overwrites.
3. **TRAP:** a delegate who presses Post by mistake. If it's on SSCE Equipment, no harm is done: tell Dan the time and he removes it. If it's on a real rig, tell Dan straight away.

### Report 1 — Daily report (WCGRRT)

Follow the Module 1 steps on screen:
1. **⊘ New Trip**, then **SSCE Equipment**.
2. Add a **📋 Daily Report Entry**, then set **Report Date** to 2026-10-11.
3. Add one equipment entry with two captioned photos.
4. **📤 Post Report.** Read the back-dated question aloud and click **OK**.
5. Read the receipt aloud.

**SAY:** "The back-dated question is there because of West Capella. On the rig it almost always means the wrong date."

### Report 2 — Surface BOP test (SSORT)

1. **⊘ New Trip**, then **SSCE Equipment**, then **Log Date** 2026-10-11.
2. Add a BOP Function Test and fill a few lines.
3. **📤 Post Report.** Point at the line under the button: **✓ Posted to the dashboard**.

**SAY:** "One report type per workspace. If SSORT asks you about two types, press Cancel."

### Report 3 — CBM inspection, Riser Adapter (SSORT)

1. **⊘ New Trip**, then Log Date 2026-10-11, then the CBM tile, then **Riser Adapter**.
2. Model and serial **TRN-11**. They're required: NOV needs them.
3. Three grades, one test pass (1) and one test fail (5), with findings.
4. Attach the sample test chart with the task number in its note.
5. **📤 Post Report.**
6. **Post to OEM:** press it only if Dan has added the SSCE Equipment guard to the CBM to OEM flow (his Saturday step 1). If he has, show the `[TEST MODE]` email to the office. If not, **don't press it.** Show the confirm box and cancel.
7. Switch a scratch tile to **Gate Valves** to show NOV's usage table (row 00 values in the manual), then ⊘ New Trip without posting.

### Report 4 — Pre-deployment checklist (SSORT)

1. **⊘ New Trip**, then Log Date 2026-10-11, then the pre-deployment checklist.
2. Set the **cavity count first** (otherwise the cavity slots say why they're missing).
3. Fill in the packer attestation and two cavity photos.
4. Show the Rev 9 additions and 🔗 **Link to plan**.
5. **📤 Post Report.**

### On the dashboard (16:35)

1. Open the Rig Visit Dashboard and filter to **SSCE Equipment**. Within ten minutes all four rows appear under 2026-10-11.
2. Open the CBM in the full-report viewer: grades, findings and the attached chart.

**SAY:** "That's the loop: the tool, the dashboard, the office. Tomorrow you'll see the rest of it."

### Close (16:45)

1. **Everyone:** open both tools and wait three seconds. If **📤 n waiting to send** shows, it means someone pressed Post with no connection. Press **Send now** and tell Dan.
2. Take questions. Point to the manual's known-errors page.
3. **After the class:** delete your four 2026-10-11 posts (and any OEM copy) from PostedReports.
