The story

I know exactly what the rotation does to you.

Two weeks on, two weeks off, for years. Long shifts, galley food, no sleep, and then home with a pocket full of money and nothing to do but drink it. I was 72 pounds heavier than I am now and I told myself it was the job.

On 2 April 2025 I stopped drinking. That was the whole plan at first: just don't drink today. Two months later I walked into a gym and started filming everything, because I didn't trust myself to remember what it had been like.

Fifteen months on, I've got the physique I never thought I'd have, a sobriety counter I check every morning, and a folder of videos that shows every step. I'm now training as a qualified personal trainer so I can coach this properly, not just tell people what worked for me.

Roughneck Fitness is for the version of me who was standing on that beach with two drinks. If that's you, I know how to get you out.

— [Coach name], founder

How it works

No fluff. A process.

  1. Apply

    Two minutes. Your rota, your kit, your goal, and whether drink is part of the picture. No judgement either way.

  2. Call

    Twenty minutes on the phone. If it's not a fit I'll say so and point you somewhere better.

  3. Plan

    Your programme lands in the app before your next hitch, built for the gym you'll actually be in.

  4. Report

    Weekly check-in: weight, photos, sessions done, how the days off went. Adjust, repeat, next rotation.
