# First-meeting checklist

Glance at this during the conversation. Say the answers out loud near the end so the recording has them; transcripts catch the chat and miss the facts.

1. Business name, exactly as they spell it. Address, phone, opening hours.
2. What they do, for whom, and what makes them different, in their words.
3. Do they have a website now? What's wrong with it? Who built it, who has the logins, what does it cost them a month?
4. Do they own a domain? Which one, and where is it registered?
5. The one thing the site must make happen: bookings, calls, enquiries, walk-ins, listings viewed.
6. Their customers' languages. Roughly what share arrive in Spanish versus English?
7. What they already have: logo, sign, menu or price list, photos, text. Photos of paper are fine.
8. Who says yes, and who will send changes later.
9. Any deadline: opening, season, event.
10. Money signal: did the launch offer land, and are they interested in Care?

Then: "I'll send a two-page proposal by tomorrow. If it's a yes, half up front and the first draft is with you inside the week."

## After the meeting

Paste the transcript here with one line: "New client, do the proposal." Add anything the recording missed. You get back the filled proposal as a Word file and PDF, ready to send. On a yes, send the deposit invoice from the template and paste the client's materials: the draft site follows.
