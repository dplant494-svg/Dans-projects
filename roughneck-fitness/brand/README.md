## Colours and type
- Steel black `#14171A`, safety orange `#FF6A13`, chalk `#E8E4DC`, mid grey `#9AA0A8`.
- Headings Barlow Condensed (heavy, uppercase); body Barlow. Both free from Google Fonts. Same as the site.
- Orange is the accent, never the main colour of a page or garment.

## Slogan
**Train smarter and harder.** (Dan's, 14 Oct 2026: a play on "work smarter, not harder". Smart programming *and* hard work, not one instead of the other.) Used on the site (hero line, footer, share text) and the share image. On clothing it works as a back print or sleeve line.

## Other lines
- Use: **"Do what you're told. Follow the process."** (the coaching ethos, credited to Shaun's system).
- Fine: "Same plan. Different day. Better you."
- Don't use: "Discipline builds freedom". It's too close to "Discipline Equals Freedom", Jocko Willink's book and merch line.

## Clothing rules (trademark)
ALL AMERICAN ROUGHNECK is a registered US trademark for clothing (see the main README). So on garments:
- Lead with the **mark** (the R) and the ethos lines.
- "Roughneck Fitness" only in full, and small (e.g. under the mark, or a neck print). Never **ROUGHNECK** alone, and never as a big back print.
- Cap: mark only. Tee: mark on the left chest; back print "FOLLOW THE PROCESS" if wanted. Hoodie: mark on the chest, ethos line on the back.
- Get the trademark check done before the shop opens.

## Never
- The fellowship's symbol (circle and triangle) in any brand graphic.
- Recolouring the mark outside the palette, stretching it, or adding effects.
