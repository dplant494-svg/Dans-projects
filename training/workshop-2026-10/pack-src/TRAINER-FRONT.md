# How to use this script

**This is your script for both days.** Read it once on Saturday, rehearse it module by module on Saturday afternoon, and run it against the clock on Sunday. On the day, keep it open beside the projector laptop.

**How each step is written:**

- **Plain numbered steps** are what you do or say, in order.
- **Bold** words are what you must say more or less as written: they are either a safety point or something people get wrong.
- `[SHOT n]` is the screen the room should see at that point. The picture is printed right under it.
- **TRAP:** is where the room will go wrong if you don't say it first.
- `[Slide n]` (Day 2) is the slide to have up.

## Say these three known errors before the room finds them (Monday, Module 1)

| KE | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **KE-06** | Four WCGRRT test records (ROV, acoustic, EDS, EHBS) hold nothing for SSCE Equipment: each shows one line instead of a form. | Show those forms on a real rig, then switch back to SSCE Equipment before posting. The script handles it in Module 2. |

| **KE-13** | A browser can keep showing an older revision from its cache. | Check the badge. Press Ctrl+F5, or close and reopen. This is the 07:45 badge check. |

- **The back-dated question in WCGRRT** is expected on your back-dated demo posts: you click OK.
