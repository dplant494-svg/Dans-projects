What changed since the 11 September chart

  • The server transfer has a date and a person: 18 October, Dan with Adam Snyder in Houston. On the 11 September chart it was "copied, ISIT have not started". The copy is refreshed on the day, so the server gets the current scanner (v2.71 today), the state files and the config.
  • Ask SACRED AI is live (13 Sep) in Seadrill Apps PROD with web search and general knowledge off. Only the Teams app approval is outstanding, submitted 16 Sep. First rig test (West Capella) the day it is allowed.
  • The database is built, by Dan, with no IT resource. SACRED DATA is a Fabric SQL database in Dan's workspace (created 1 Oct; 22 tables, 6 views, one needs_attention definition). The scanner writes a normalised export on every run (schema 2: reports, rig checks, CBM, AAB, precharge requests and issued sheets, Help Centre records, SSCE requests, 21 tables) and a Dataflow Gen2 loads it under Dan's own sign-in. Nothing to provision, no secret, nothing installed. The January row is no longer an IT ask: the only cost item is Fabric capacity on Lee's cost centre when the trial ends about 12 November, and an app registration later if the scanner is to write directly.
  • Three more notification loops exist: CBM reports to NOV (proven 22 Sep, HTML attachment 28 Sep, go-live email to NOV in October), the AAB loop on the Seadrill Bulletin Board (proven end to end in test mode 28 Sep; pilot under a documented deviation with the MOC and HAZID while DIR-37-0161 is revised), and the TSC Help Centre (installed 29 Sep, flow 30 Sep). All share one pattern: post, scanner, page, notification with a dashboard link, receipt.
  • Test mode is built into every notification flow: while on, only the office list is mailed and the subject says so. No rig or NOV address receives a test.
  • Monitoring: the scanner writes its last-successful-scan stamp on every complete run (since 14 Sep). The hourly Digests freshness alert promised to IT is built in early October, before the move, and keeps watching after it.
  • Training: the two-day class in Houston, 19 and 20 October, the two days after the transfer: reporting tools on the Monday; dashboards, precharge process, AAB and Help Centre on the Tuesday. Packs final by 12 October.
  • Scanner today: v2.71, no modules, nothing installed, reads three SharePoint libraries through OneDrive sync, does a full parse only when something changed (about a second otherwise). The service account needs read on the three report libraries, write on Digests and on the two IIS folders. No secrets, nothing to rotate.
  • Removed from the chart: the hosted application (Dan, 11 Sep). The programme ends with ISIT accepting the pages, scanner and task as a supported service.
  • Draft 3, 1 October: the database rows are redrawn. M1b, M3 and the export half of M6 are done; M9 is no longer "first IT resource". The ISIT asks on this chart are now only the service account, the Teams approval, the 10 October decisions and the ORR configuration work.
  • Draft 2, 1 October: ISIT's Operational Readiness Review was gapped on 30 September (3 of 30 met, 26 dated with owners, 1 not applicable); its dated actions are a new section on page 1, with the ISIT decisions due 10 October, the 18 October handover list, the security submission by 31 October and the ManageEngine, CMDB and Service Desk configuration by 6 November. SSORT 153 is live and the test records reach NOV (Part D3 proven 30 Sep). The class sits inside Lee Arnold's WCE SME workshop, 19 to 22 October.
