## What we need from you: the create page, twenty minutes of it

1. **A walkthrough script of creating an advisory** on your Rev 10 page: the identification panel
   (number, title, category, dates, eDocs, Maximo parent, SFI), the evidence checkbox, the rigs, the
   originator, the three sections, the bulletin attachment and the photographs, Preview, Post.
   Twenty to thirty steps, a screenshot for each that needs one, on a TEST advisory for SSCE
   Equipment or West Vela in test mode.
2. **Revising and withdrawing**: Load AAB file, raise the revision, the re-acknowledgement checkbox,
   the withdrawal checkbox; five steps each.
3. **The three mistakes to show**: a post with no bulletin (what the email carries instead), a
   revision posted at revision 0 (the page refuses the withdrawal), an advisory with evidence asked
   for and nothing attached at the rig (the rig page refuses).
4. **Three questions with answers** for the workbook, and one slide's worth of content on "what
   Technical Services put into an advisory and why".
5. **Your ten minutes of the fleet compliance review** as you will do it live: open an acknowledged
   rig, read both crews, the evidence, close with a comment. We demonstrate the page; you say what a
   satisfactory acknowledgement looks like and what you send back.
