Well Control Equipment — Technical Services

Dashboards and Reporting Tools — three-day class

Agenda and timings · 08:00 to 17:00 each day · seven teaching hours a day · week plan item 22 · draft 23 September 2026

Each day: 08:00 start · 10:00 break (15 min) · 12:00 lunch (60 min) · 14:30 break (15 min) · 17:00 close. Precharge theory and process take the whole morning of day 2. No trainee ever posts: reports are saved locally, the trainer drops them into a training folder that a second scanner configuration reads and deploys to the sandbox site, so the fleet dashboard never sees them.
