# Logo prompt: Seadrill CORE (Central Operations & Resource Environment)

Paste everything between the lines into the image or design tool. Attach the Seadrill star (`branding/argo-navis/`, taken from the Seadrill Brand Guidelines) and, if the tool accepts them, the Argo Navis logos as a style reference.

---

Design a logo for **Seadrill CORE**, which stands for **Central Operations & Resource Environment**. It is Seadrill's company-wide application for Technical Services: one place to find the technical standards and requirements for every rig. You open a rig on a spinning globe and drill into region, vessel, discipline, SFI equipment group, maintenance and operating requirements, and regulatory and company requirements.

**Brand: follow Seadrill's corporate identity exactly.**
- **Colours:**
  - Seadrill Blue **#002C77**: primary, for the wordmark and main shapes.
  - Seadrill Yellow **#EDB71E**: accent, for the star only.
  - Light Blue **#0082C0**: secondary detail.
  - Off White **#F4F7FA** and Charcoal **#1F2D3D**: backgrounds and text.
  - No other colours, no gradients beyond a subtle one inside the globe, no glows, no 3D bevels.
- **The Seadrill star:** use the supplied Seadrill star symbol, in Seadrill Yellow, unchanged in shape. Do not redraw it, distort it, outline it or rotate it. It is the one gold element.
- **Typography:** a clean geometric sans-serif matching Seadrill's Meta Pro, for example Meta Pro, or Source Sans / Inter as a stand-in.
  - "CORE" in bold capitals with generous letter spacing.
  - "Seadrill" set smaller above or before it, in Seadrill Blue.
  - The strapline "Central Operations & Resource Environment" small, in Charcoal or Light Blue, under the wordmark.

**The symbol.** A simple, flat **globe** in Seadrill Blue and Light Blue:
- two or three meridian and latitude lines, no continents in detail;
- the **Seadrill star** sitting on or just above the globe like a location pin, marking a rig: one gold star on the globe;
- **optional:** a thin orbit ring around the globe, suggesting rotation and a central hub.

It must read as "the centre of everything, worldwide", and still be clean at 16 px.

**Deliver these versions:**
1. **Primary horizontal:** globe symbol on the left, "Seadrill CORE" and the strapline on the right. On white (light version) and on Seadrill Blue #002C77 (dark version: wordmark in white, star still yellow).
2. **Stacked:** the symbol above the wordmark, light and dark.
3. **App icon:** the symbol only, globe plus star, on a Seadrill Blue rounded square, at 512 / 256 / 64 / 32 / 16 px, with no text at small sizes.
4. **Favicon:** the star on a small globe, readable at 16 px.

**Formats:** SVG plus transparent PNG for each, with clear space around the logo of at least the height of the star.

**Avoid:**
- clip art;
- realistic earth textures;
- oil-rig drawings or drilling derricks in the logo;
- any colour other than the five above;
- altering the Seadrill star;
- the full Seadrill corporate logo itself. This is a sub-brand lock-up like Argo Navis; Seadrill Communications approves the final.

**Mood:** precise, global, trustworthy, engineering-grade. It sits beside the Argo Navis logo as part of the same family.
