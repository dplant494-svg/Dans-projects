# Explainer video for the workshop: the WCE planning loop, from scope to deployment (Claude Motion)

**For:** the WCE SME workshop, Houston, 19 to 22 October 2026 (day 2's loop module, and the open workshop days)
**Length:** about 90 seconds, no voice track needed (captions on screen; Dan or Lee narrate live if they want)
**Made with:** Claude Motion (beta; an admin switches it on under Organization settings, Artifacts). Motion animates
our own text, charts and images; nothing is generated footage. Exported as MP4 for the deck.
**Brand:** Seadrill blue `#002C77`, yellow `#FFD100`, light blue `#0091DA`, white; the DL-1 Master Plan logo
(`branding/dl1-master-plan-wide.png`) and the SACRED wordmark.

## The one idea

One press on the rig starts a loop that plans, reviews, freezes, executes, evidences and closes a BOP end-of-well
maintenance campaign, with every document filed and every person told, and nobody typing anything twice.
