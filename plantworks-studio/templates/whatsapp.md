## Replying live in Spanish without speaking it

1. Samsung Keyboard → Chat assist → Chat translation on, Spanish pack downloaded. The translate bar above the keyboard turns incoming Spanish into English and your English reply into Spanish.
2. Fallback: Google Translate "Tap to translate" (copy the message, bubble appears, type reply, copy back).
3. Anything longer or important: paste the message into the Claude session and send back the reply it gives.
