# Seadrill CBM inspection reports: opening the attachment

**For:** NOV CBM review team · **From:** Seadrill Technical Services, Well Control Engineering
**Date:** 28 September 2026

Seadrill's rigs now send their Condition Based Monitoring (CBM) inspection reports to NOV
automatically, as soon as the inspection is posted. Each report arrives as one email with one
attachment.

## The email

- **Subject:** `[Seadrill CBM Report for OEM review] CBM Report - <rig> - <equipment> - <date>`
- **From:** the Seadrill Well Control Engineering mailbox. Replies go to the Seadrill people in
  copy, not to that mailbox.
- **Attachment:** `Seadrill_CBM_<rig>_<equipment>_<date>.html`, the inspection report exactly
  as the rig's reporting tool renders it: header, task table with the 1 to 5 grades, comments,
  and the photographs, all inside the one file. Nothing to download separately, no link to
  follow, no login. The dashboard link in the email body is for Seadrill readers on the
  internal network and will not open outside it.

## Opening the report

**Windows or Mac.** Double-click the attachment, or save it and double-click the file. It opens
in your default browser (Edge, Chrome, Safari, Firefox all render it the same). No internet
connection is needed: the file is self-contained.

**iPhone or iPad.** Tap the attachment. Mail and Outlook show it in place. Tap the share icon to
save it to Files.

**Android.** Tap the attachment. Outlook or Gmail open it in the browser; some phones ask which
app to use, choose Chrome.

## Printing or saving as PDF

Open the report in the browser, press **Ctrl+P** (Mac: **Cmd+P**), and choose **Save as PDF** (or
**Microsoft Print to PDF**) as the printer. The page breaks and colours are kept.

## If it does not open

- **Outlook shows the attachment but nothing happens on double-click:** right-click it,
  **Save As**, save to the desktop, then double-click the saved file.
- **The browser shows a blank page or code:** the file was saved without its `.html`
  extension. Rename it so it ends in `.html` and open it again.
- **The attachment is missing or was removed by mail security:** tell Seadrill (the people in
  copy on the email) and the report is re-sent, as a PDF if your mail system prefers it.
- **Grade colours:** green 1 and 2, orange 3 (acceptable with findings, monitor), red 4 and 5.
  They follow NOV's own grade level evaluation guide.
