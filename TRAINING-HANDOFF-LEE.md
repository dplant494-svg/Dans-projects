# Training class handoff — what Dan has to ask Lee for

**To:** Lee Arnold (WCE Manager) · **From:** the dashboard session, via Dan
**Date:** 29 September 2026 · **Class:** week plan item 22, agenda `WCE-Training-Class-Agenda.pdf`

Three parts of the two-day class rest on tools and records that are yours, not Dan's, and the
dashboard session has no access to them. This is the list, module by module, with what is needed
for each so the pack is complete.

## 1. The COC dashboard and the SSCE request cycle (Day 2, 13:00 to 14:30, about twenty minutes)

The class shows the SSCE Requests Dashboard (ours) and **the WCE COC Dashboard** (yours: the
Certification Tracker and the CoC Register behind it, the Submit Request button, the approve or
deny with comment, the "Allocated" grey-out that the scanner writes back). Needed:

- A walkthrough script of raising a request from the COC dashboard and seeing its decision come
  back, twenty steps, screenshots on a test item, with the three mistakes a rig makes.
- One paragraph each on what the Certification Tracker and the CoC Register are, who keeps them,
  and what "certified", "allocated" and "expired" mean on the screen.
- The current build of `Seadrill_WCE_COC_Dashboard.html` the class will use, frozen for the week.

## 2. SPARC, and where the estate is heading (Day 2, 14:45 to 16:00, about fifteen minutes of it)

"What is coming" ends with where the estate is heading in the next six months. SPARC (the Seadrill
Parts And Recertification Catalogue) and its link to Maximo are the part of that picture the dashboard
session knows only from your 14 September briefing. Needed:

- Five to eight points for one slide: what SPARC is, what it holds, how it links to Maximo, what a
  rig will see from it, and when.
- Whether you want to present those fifteen minutes yourself. If so the slide is yours to check
  and the script is one paragraph.

## 3. The MOCs and the signatures (Day 2, 08:00 to 10:00, twenty minutes inside the precharge module; and the AAB MOC)

The precharge process module ends with "who signs, and the MOC". The MOC lives in Synergi, and the
AAB loop is a pilot under deviation using the MOC and the HAZID. Needed:

- The precharge MOC: its number, the change it covers, the signatures the process requires, and what
  a rig does when a precharge has to change outside it. One page.
- The AAB pilot MOC and HAZID: the case numbers and the one-line status the class should hear
  ("pilot under deviation, HAZID H1 to H8 closed or open as follows").
- The pre-deployment MOC (week plan item 23) if it is through by the class date; otherwise the class
  says it is in progress.

## Form and timing

Markdown plus PNG screenshots (1600 px, light mode, test items and SSCE Equipment only), the way every
handoff travels. Draft to Dan two weeks before the class date, one review round, final a week before.
The dashboard session lays all of it out on the Seadrill template and writes the trainer's script
around it.
