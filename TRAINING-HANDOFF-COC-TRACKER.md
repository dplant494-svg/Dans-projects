# Training class handoff — the WCE COC Dashboard (the Certification Tracker), Day 2

**To:** the session that maintains the WCE COC Dashboard / Certification Tracker (Lee Arnold's tool)
**From:** the dashboard session, via Dan · **Date:** 29 September 2026
**Class:** week plan item 22, agenda `WCE-Training-Class-Agenda.pdf` draft 3

The COC Dashboard and the Certification Tracker are one tool, and this handoff is for it alone;
SPARC has its own. Two places in the class:

## 1. Day 2, 13:00 to 14:30: the tool itself, about twenty-five minutes

Shown beside the SSCE Requests Dashboard (ours), which is where its requests are decided. Needed:

1. **What it is**, one paragraph for the slide: the certification tracker for well control
   equipment, what it tracks (which certificates, which equipment, which expiry rule), who keeps it
   and how often it is updated, and what "certified", "allocated" and "expired" mean on the screen.
2. **A walkthrough script**, twenty steps with screenshots on a test item: find an item, read its
   certification state, see what an expiring certificate looks like; then **Submit Request** for a
   spare, and what comes back after the decision on the requests page (approved or denied with the
   comment, and the "Allocated" grey-out the scanner writes back).
3. **The three mistakes a rig makes** on it and what the tool does about each.
4. **The current build** of `Seadrill_WCE_COC_Dashboard.html` the class will use, frozen for the
   week, and the test items it is safe to demonstrate on.

## 2. Day 2, 14:45 to 15:30: its place in the loop, about five minutes

The loop module shows how a request raised on the COC Dashboard reaches the requests page, is
decided, and is written back. Needed: one paragraph on what the tool sends and receives today (the
request record, the decision, the allocation), and what you would connect next, in your order.

## In what form

Markdown plus PNG screenshots (1600 px, light mode, test items and SSCE Equipment only), numbered to
match the script, the way every handoff travels. Three workbook questions with answers per module,
and one slide's worth of points per module; the dashboard session lays it out on the Seadrill
template and writes the trainer's script around it. Draft to Dan two weeks before the class date,
one review round, final a week before. Nothing is posted from training and nothing in the tool
changes for the class.

Not needed from you: the SSCE Requests Dashboard, the scanner's write-back, the COC review copy;
ours, and documented in `SSCE-REQUESTS-INTEGRATION-CONTRACT.md`.
