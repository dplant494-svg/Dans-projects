# The Fifth Quarter: social media from scratch

A guide for Mark. About an hour to set up, then the posts are ready to go.

**The rule underneath all of it:** the restaurant owns its own accounts. They're created by Mark, on the restaurant's email, and Plantworks is given access to help. Nobody shares a password.

---

## Part 1. Set up the accounts (about 45 minutes)

### 1. Facebook Page (do this first)
Facebook needs a real person behind every business page, so it's created from **Mark's own Facebook profile**.

1. On Facebook, open the menu and tap **Pages**, then **Create a Page**.
2. Name: **The Fifth Quarter**. Category: **Restaurant**.
3. Add the profile picture (`posts/profile-picture.png` in this pack) and a cover photo (post 1 works).
4. In the page details, add the website `https://the5thquarter.co.uk` and the email `info@the5thquarter.co.uk`.

### 2. Instagram
1. Download Instagram and sign up with **info@the5thquarter.co.uk**. Not a personal email: the account then belongs to the business.
2. Username: try **the5thquarter** first, then **thefifthquarter**, then **the5thquarterlambton**. Use the same name everywhere.
3. Settings, Account type and tools, **Switch to professional account**, choose **Business**, category **Restaurant**.
4. Connect it to the Facebook Page when it asks.
5. Profile picture: `posts/profile-picture.png`.
6. Name field: **The Fifth Quarter · Lambton Park** (this is the bit people search).
7. Bio, ready to paste:
   ```
   Restaurant · Bar · Delicatessen
   Lions Gate, Lambton Park
   Named for the Five Quarter seam
   Opening spring 2027
   ```
8. Website: `https://the5thquarter.co.uk`
9. Contact options: email info@the5thquarter.co.uk. Add the phone when there is one.
10. Turn on **two-factor authentication** (Settings, Accounts Centre, Password and security). Keep the backup codes somewhere safe.

### 3. Give Plantworks access (no passwords)
1. Go to **business.facebook.com** (Meta Business Suite) on Mark's login.
2. Settings, **People**, **Add people**.
3. Add Dan's email, with access to **content, messages and insights** for the Facebook Page and Instagram. Not full control.
4. Dan accepts the invite and logs in as himself from then on. Mark can remove the access at any time.

### 4. Claim the handle everywhere else
Even if they won't be used yet, so nobody else takes them: **TikTok** and **X**, same username, same picture.

### 5. Google Business Profile (the most important listing for a restaurant)
This is what puts The Fifth Quarter on Google Maps with photos, opening hours and, later, the booking link.
1. Go to **business.google.com**, sign in with the restaurant's Google account (create one with info@ if needed).
2. Add the business: **The Fifth Quarter**, category **Restaurant**, address **Lions Gate, Lambton Park, Chester-le-Street DH3 4DT**.
3. Google verifies by post, phone or a short video at the address.
4. Google lets you set a future opening date, but only a few months ahead, so **finish this in early 2027** if it won't accept spring 2027 yet.
5. When it's verified, add Dan as a **Manager** (Settings, Managers), not Owner.

## Part 2. The launch grid (nine posts)

The nine images are in `posts/`, numbered in the order to post them. Instagram shows the newest first, so after all nine the profile reads like `grid-preview.png`: a navy-and-parchment checkerboard with Find Us, The Bar and the Head Chef across the top row.

**When to post:** posts 1, 2 and 3 on the first day, so the profile never looks empty. Then one every two days. After post 9, post **Reel 1** ("Beneath our feet", in `../reel-1/`) with music added in Instagram.

**After posting:** pin post 1, post 7 and the reel (tap the three dots on a post, Pin to your profile).

**Every post:** add the location **Lambton Park**, paste the caption below, and write the alt text (Advanced settings, Accessibility) as one plain sentence describing the image.

## Part 3. After the launch

- **Reply to every comment and message within the day.** In the first months that is the whole algorithm.
- **Share each Instagram post to the Facebook Page** too (Meta Business Suite does both at once).
- **Behind the scenes beats polish.** The build, the kitchen going in, deliveries, the team in training, tasting days. Phone video is fine.
- **Real photography before opening.** When the food is being cooked, book a proper photographer for a day: it supplies the website, Google and Instagram for a year.
- **When Toast Tables is live,** add its booking link to the Instagram **Reserve** button and the Google profile.
- Don't buy followers or run giveaways for follows; they bring the wrong people and Instagram shows the posts to fewer of the right ones.

## Before posting, check
- **Post 7**: David approves all three slides and the caption. They're written from his own bio (8 Oct).
- **Post 6**: Sunday menu as shown, or change it to something he's sure of.
- **Post 4**: "seven days from 9am" is still the plan.

The posts are built from `src/grid.html`; changes are quick to remake.
