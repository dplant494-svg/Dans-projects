Story

Discipline builds freedom.

The Praetorians were the standard everyone else was measured against. That's the bar.

Praetorian Fitness makes a small number of things and makes them properly. No slogans across the chest, no seasonal drops for the sake of it, no fabric that gives up after a summer. One mark, worn small, by people who don't need to explain what they do.

Train. Perform. Belong. The order matters. The work comes first, the results follow, and the rest is who you find yourself standing next to.
